A little help applying, every morning.

Job Apply searches LinkedIn for roles that fit, fills in the application forms with your answers, and keeps track of every one — while you sleep. You stay in control: it starts in prepare mode, walking each application to the final button and stopping. You press Submit.

How it works, honestly
  • Your real browser. It uses your own logged-in Chrome, at a human pace. No bots-behind-the-scenes tricks — that's what keeps your account safe.
  • Your own Claude subscription does the reading and typing. Nothing is submitted anywhere without the prepare-mode review unless you later switch it to live mode yourself.
  • Your data stays on this Mac. Profile, CVs, application history — all local files.
  • For personal use. This automates part of job hunting for you; it respects LinkedIn's limits and applies at a modest, human-scale pace.
Have these handy
  • Your LinkedIn sign-in (you'll type it once, in Chrome yourself)
  • Your CV as a PDF
  • ~15 minutes
